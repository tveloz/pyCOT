#sps_RN_MLM_V02

# Nut: Phloem sap nutrients--Non-essential organic nutrients and sugars absorbed from phloem sap.
# Nut_int: Internalized nutrients--Intracellular nutrient pool assimilated and processed by endosymbionts.
# B: Buchnera aphidicola--Obligate intracellular primary endosymbiont.
# H: Secondary endosymbionts--Facultative endosymbionts (H. defensa / F. symbiotica) conferring resilience.
# A_eaa: Essential amino acids--Protein precursor pool synthesized by Buchnera.
# P_hsp: Heat-shock proteins--Molecular chaperones expressed under thermal stress.
# W_nit: Nitrogenous waste--Host catabolic byproducts recycled into nutrients.
# E_fit: Host fitness reserves--Cytosolic energetic pool sustaining host growth and fecundity.
# C_lip: Cytosolic lipids & sugars--Nutritional substrate assimilated for endosymbiont maintenance.
# T_tox: Anti-parasitoid toxins--Secondary metabolites synthesized by secondary endosymbionts.
# T_sigma: Thermal stress index--Environmental temperature perturbation modulating kinetic rates.
# A_vul: Vulnerable aphid phenotype--Nymph or adult susceptible to predation and parasitism.
# A_apt: Apterous aphid morph--Unwinged adult with high basal reproductive capacity.
# A_ala: Alate aphid morph--Winged adult adapted for long-distance spatial dispersal.
# A_def: Defended aphid morph--Host possessing endosymbiont-mediated parasitoid defense.
# A_par: Parasitoid-infected aphid--Host carrying endoparasitoid egg or growing larva.
# M_mum: Parasitoid mummy--Sclerotized aphid carcass housing developing pupa.
# H_dew: Honeydew excretion--Sugar-rich metabolic byproduct excreted during feeding.
# Pl: Healthy plant biomass--Basal vegetative host tissue (Fabaceae) fixing CO2.
# Pl_dam: Damaged plant tissue--Foliar tissue undergoing cellular stress from herbivory.
# P_p: Parasitoid wasp--Adult female parasitoid wasp (Aphidius ervi).
# D: Generalist predator--Higher-trophic arthropod predator.
# V_voc: Volatile organic compounds--Airborne infochemical signals emitted by damaged plant tissue.
# F_fun: Saprophytic fungi--Fungal colonies developing on honeydew deposits.
# S_alm: Alarm pheromone--Volatile infochemical released during predator attacks.
